# Week 10 - Clustering Workbook

This is an ungraded in-class workbook covering retail-customer clustering,
distance and scaling choices, k-means assignment and centroid updates,
initialization, inertia, silhouette score, cluster profiles, and business
interpretation.

You are encouraged to review the workbook before or after class at your own
pace. We will work through the central ideas and selected sections together
during class.
